About HEAL

To help people search for, evaluate, and use generative AI for health information, safely, critically, and for life.

HEAL researches how people form trust in AI-generated health answers, then builds the practical skills to search well, evaluate rigorously, and act wisely, skills that hold up as both AI tools and health information keep changing.

  • Search: form the right question and know where to look.
  • Evaluate: check claims, sources, and AI-generated confidence.
  • Use: apply findings critically, ethically, and safely.
