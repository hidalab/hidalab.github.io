What Linh should do this week

  1. Keep doing the espresso. It's the moat. Reviewers in five different languages independently call it the best in the area.
  2. Staff one extra barista from 11:30am–1:30pm. The wait-time complaints cluster specifically at lunch, not all day. A small, targeted fix.
  3. Add a $40 WiFi range extender on the back patio. Five customers mentioned it in fifteen reviews. The cheapest fix on this list.
One trade-off worth knowing. DeepSeek is Chinese-made. For some US businesses that raises real questions about geopolitics and supply chain that are worth taking seriously — even when, like here, the model runs entirely on Linh's own laptop and no data leaves the building.
Why this works

Reading and clustering text in multiple languages is inside the frontier — and self-hosted AI changes the economics.

Six months ago, a small business owner with this problem had three options: pay a translator, ignore the non-English reviews, or send customer data to a third party. DeepSeek being open-weights and runnable on a laptop adds a fourth option: do it yourself, for free, without anyone else seeing the data. That's not a small change for small businesses.
