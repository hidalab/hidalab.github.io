ANALYZING…

Why Variant B won the click.

Looking at the creative side by side, three differences stand out:

01 · Contrast

Strongest color contrast

The red-orange gradient against white text has the highest luminance gap. Eyes lock on first in the feed.

02 · Words

Concrete in three words

"50% OFF" lands instantly. A and C ask the reader to interpret the message before deciding to click.

03 · Urgency

"Ends tonight" creates a clock

Time pressure converts interest into action. A and C have no urgency signal at all.

But here's what the chart hides.

!Look at week 2

B's customers don't stick around.

Cheap clicks are easy. Customers who stay are not. When we trace the same users to their 14-day retention numbers, the story flips.

VERDICT

B wins on the dashboard. C wins on the customer.

The 50%-off ad pulled in deal-hunters who churned the moment the promotion ended. The "built to last ten years" ad attracted people who actually wanted a durable coffee machine, and they're still buying refills.

Don't kill C because of the click-through rate. Move more budget to C, and reserve B for clearance moments only.

Why this works

Reading creative + numbers together is exactly what AI is good at.

Numbers alone said "B won." Looking at the image, the copy, and the downstream retention together gives a different answer. Marketing teams used to need a creative director, a data analyst, and a few days to do this. Gemini does it in a paragraph — and the team's job is to decide whether to trust it.
