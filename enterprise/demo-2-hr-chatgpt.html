ChatGPT handles the questions HR already answered last week.

HR fields about 120 questions a week. Most are repeats: vacation, expenses, parental leave, remote work. The answers are sitting in the handbook. But finding them takes time, and responses lag a day or two.

The job: triage, not replace

ChatGPT reads the company's actual policies. It cites a source for every answer. When a question is sensitive, it refuses and routes the employee to a real person. Every conversation is logged so HR can spot weak policies.

Why this works

The hardest questions get more attention, not less.

The 95 routine questions don't disappear — they get answered in seconds with a citation. HR's afternoon is free to actually focus on the 5 that need a human: harassment, family disputes, accommodation cases. That's the trade. AI handles the lookup; people handle the judgment.
