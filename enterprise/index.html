Four short demos, one per business area. Each one shows what an AI tool actually does on a real task — and where it would fail if you weren't paying attention. Click any card to begin.

01 · ACCOUNTING

Checking a vendor's bill

Tool: Claude

Hundreds of bills come in every week. An employee checks each one against the purchase order. Most are fine. A few aren't. Six minutes per bill becomes fifteen seconds.
