The Problem

When health information meets artificial intelligence.

Obesity is one of the most common chronic health conditions in the United States. At the same time, generative AI is becoming an increasingly common source of health information. Where these two trends meet, important questions arise.

40.3%
of U.S. adults age 20 and older had obesity during August 2021–August 2023.
Source: CDC, National Center for Health Statistics
34%
of U.S. adults have used AI chatbots for at least one health-related reason.
Source: Pew Research Center, 2026

AI can provide answers. But can people trust them?

People managing obesity may turn to generative AI for advice on diet, exercise, weight-loss medications, and related health concerns. While these tools can make information easier to access, their responses may also be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for an individual's health needs. Understanding how people evaluate and act on these responses is essential for supporting reliable health information seeking.

Our Research

What HEAL aims to achieve.

HEAL, funded by the Network of the National Library of Medicine (NNLM), Region 2, studies how adults living with obesity use generative AI tools to find and evaluate health information.

Through a national survey, we explore what people ask AI, which answers they trust, and how those answers inform their decisions. Our research also examines privacy concerns and people's confidence in recognizing unreliable health information.

In addition to academic publications, our findings will inform workshops and educational resources for local communities and the public, helping people find and use reliable health information.

01

Understand AI Use

Investigate how adults living with obesity use AI tools to search for information about nutrition, physical activity, weight management, and related health concerns.

02

Examine Trust and Evaluation

Explore how people assess the credibility of AI-generated responses, recognize potentially misleading information, and navigate privacy concerns when seeking health advice.

03

Strengthen AI Literacy

Translate research findings into educational resources and workshops that help communities critically evaluate and responsibly use AI-generated health information.

From research to real-world impact.

HEAL connects research with practical education. By understanding how people seek, trust, and use AI-generated health information, we aim to support stronger health and AI literacy and more informed decision-making.
