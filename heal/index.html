The Problem

When health information meets artificial intelligence.

Obesity is one of the most common chronic conditions in the United States, affecting about 40% of adults. At the same time, generative AI is quickly becoming a trusted resource for health information, with one in three Americans now using AI chatbots for health questions. Where these two trends meet, important questions arise. People managing obesity may turn to AI for advice on diet, exercise, and weight-loss medications. The answers can be helpful, but they can also be inaccurate or incomplete.

Our Research

What HEAL aims to achieve.

HEAL studies how people with obesity use generative AI for health information. Through a national survey, we explore what people ask AI, which answers they trust, and how those answers inform their decisions. In addition to academic publications, our findings will inform workshops and educational resources for local communities and the public, helping people find and use reliable health information.
