About HEAL

Helping people search for, evaluate, and use AI-generated health information critically, ethically, and safely.

HEAL examines how people search for health information using generative AI, how they evaluate the credibility of AI-generated responses, and how they use this information to inform their health decisions.

With a particular focus on adults living with obesity, the project investigates how individuals use AI tools to obtain information about weight management, nutrition, physical activity, and related health concerns. It also explores their experiences, trust, privacy concerns, and confidence in evaluating AI-generated health information.

Through this research, HEAL aims to develop educational resources that help individuals find reliable health information, critically evaluate AI-generated responses, and make informed health decisions. These skills remain important as AI technologies and health information continue to evolve.

  • Search: form the right question and know where to look.
  • Evaluate: check claims, sources, and AI-generated confidence.
  • Use: apply findings critically, ethically, and safely.

This project is funded by the Network of the National Library of Medicine (NNLM), Region 2. The project is led by Ehsan Mohammadi and a multidisciplinary research team working to improve health information literacy.

Why Obesity Matters

Supporting access to reliable obesity-related health information.

Obesity is a major public health concern in the United States. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), obesity is associated with increased risks of several chronic health conditions, including type 2 diabetes, heart disease, and certain cancers.

People seeking information about obesity and weight management may encounter recommendations from many sources, including generative AI tools. Understanding how individuals find, evaluate, and use this information is important for supporting informed health decisions.

HEAL addresses this need by examining people's experiences with AI-generated obesity-related information and identifying opportunities to strengthen health and AI literacy.
